# Art and motion — Astral 0.9

Custom artwork uses optional `contours` on a Graphic: closed arrays of local `{x,y}` points. This overrides the built-in shape outline. Opposite winding creates holes. Paths use the same position, scale, rotation, opacity, draw-on and live joint/bone bindings as existing graphics. Existing shapes default to no custom contours and retain their original outlines. Custom paths require schema 7; older projects still load. Paths are bounded to 512 contours, 16,384 points and finite local coordinates within ±8192. The desktop and native export use the same path geometry. Shapes outside the camera are culled before rasterization/tessellation, including scenery in tracking shots that extend beyond the original canvas.

Agents can provide `contours` to `add_motion_graphic`, or use `add_graphic` / `replace_graphic` through a revision-checked command batch. Treat an asset as coherent geometry: furniture needs a plan, visible thickness, supports, floor contact and a consistent perspective. The new production uses original, editable native paths, not downloaded art or bitmap stand-ins.

`animate_character` now derives omitted walk/run travel from rig leg size, clip duration and project FPS. Run uses approximately three leg lengths per second; walk uses 1.2. Explicit distance overrides this. Desktop **Natural pace** uses the same helper; disable it for manual travel. Gait starts avoid an instant pelvis-height change, stance feet counteract root travel, and the torso leans into a run. These are deterministic rig tools, not a learned motion model or terrain simulation.

The After the Siren production is in `productions/after-the-siren`. Its plan and story rules are in `shot-plan.json` and `STORYBOARD.md`; the native document also contains the editable board. The relay powers the coach and locks the exit; Mina cuts two interlocks while Ren holds the coach away from her. Enemies have distinct identities and defeat states. The arm bandage and torn scarf persist after treatment. Actions follow continuous trajectories and explicit contact targets. Chases traverse the environment at a running pace; they are not short translations under fast foot loops. Scenes use original aerial exchanges, charged strikes, a beam clash and short hit holds inspired by action-anime staging, without copied characters or scenes.

Verification: `cargo test --workspace --release`, `node scripts/check-after-siren.mjs` and the production's `MOTION-REVIEW.json`. Review moving previews in addition to geometric checks. Full encode/decode success does not certify artistic quality.
