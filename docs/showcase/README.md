# Astral animation studies

These are real Astral renders and editable `.astral` projects, generated by [showcase.rs](../../crates/astral/examples/showcase.rs). They demonstrate specific tools, not an automatic production-quality film claim. The scenes and artwork were created with AI coding assistance. No third-party film frames or character assets are used.

| Study | Try editing | Project |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contact / 01 | Six fight beats, authored pose keys, contact markers, separate impacts and arena graphics. Project: 960 × 540 at 60 FPS. | [contact.astral](projects/contact.astral) |
| Soft / 02 | Embedded raster artwork, animated transform keys and five deformation pins. | [soft.astral](projects/soft.astral) |
| Voice / 03 | Editable transcript, offline system-voice WAV and independently editable mouth cues. | [voice.astral](projects/voice.astral) |

Download the **showcase ZIP** from the same release as the application, extract it, then use **Open project**. Keep `assets` next to the projects so the two audio studies can find their WAV files. Downloading only `.astral` is sufficient for the self-contained raster study. GIF previews have no sound and use 24 FPS; open the original or play the MP4 for audio/full timing.

Test a copy: change one fight pose, paint frame 12 of Soft without changing frame 13, and replace a mouth cue in Voice. Save, reopen, and check undo/redo. Effects, camera movement and intentional strike overlap can trigger review warnings; inspect the moving result.

To reproduce on Windows with installed offline voices and FFmpeg:

```powershell
$env:ASTRAL_FFMPEG = 'C:\Tools\ffmpeg\bin\ffmpeg.exe'
cargo run -p astral --release --example showcase -- docs/showcase/projects --render
```

The voice depends on the installed system voice and can sound different on another PC. The generator is Windows-specific for that example. Handcrafted artwork and synthesized impact audio are under the repository's GPL-3.0-or-later license; no system voice engine is bundled.
